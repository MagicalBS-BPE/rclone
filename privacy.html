Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Purpose

BPE Drive Backup is an internal backup utility using rclone to copy authorized Google Drive files to local or network-attached storage.

Google User Data

The utility requests read-only access to Google Drive files accessible to the authenticated user.

Data Usage and Storage

Google Drive data is used solely for backup and archival purposes. Files are copied to storage controlled by the organization. OAuth credentials are stored locally in the rclone configuration.

Data Sharing

The utility does not intentionally transmit Google Drive file contents to advertising services or data brokers. Data may be stored on authorized organizational storage systems.

Data Retention

Backup copies are retained according to the organization's applicable backup and retention policies.

Access and Revocation

Users can revoke the application's Google Drive access through their Google Account security settings.
